## Problem Statement

ECO-BRIDGE KAWEMPE (a 21-member environmental CBO in Kawempe Division, Kampala, mid-registration with KCCA) has no web presence. This blocks verification by the two audiences whose scrutiny matters most right now: the KCCA registration body and any funder doing due diligence before a grant.

## Demand Evidence

Two concrete signals, not aspiration: (1) KCCA/funder-facing registration requires a verifiable web presence — a real deadline tied to the active CBO registration; (2) partners and beneficiaries have tried to find the org online and failed — "Nothing at all" is the current workaround (no site, no consistent social presence).

## Status Quo

No website, no consistent social media. Verification currently happens only through physically handed-over Word documents and in-person introductions.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

Three audiences, confirmed together: (1) the founder/Chairperson fielding funder and partner questions, (2) the KCCA registration/board officer verifying legitimacy, (3) the funder program officer doing due diligence. The narrowest wedge: a site that answers "is this organization real and well-run?" in under a minute, backed by the org's own governance documents rather than marketing copy.

## Constraints

- Volunteer-run CBO, no paid staff, near-zero budget (fees are UGX 25,000/member).
- Long-term maintenance needs to sit with the Secretary (constitutionally responsible for records/reports), not a developer.
- Legal name in flux: ECO-BRIDGE AFRICA → ECO-BRIDGE → ECO-BRIDGE KAWEMPE (current, KCCA-advised, registration still pending).

## Premises

1. The site's primary job is credibility/verification for KCCA, funders, and the board — not donation conversion (donations are secondary for now).
2. The org's own governance documents (Constitution, Work Plan, Activity Report) are the core content asset — surfaced as real, verifiable content, not summarized into generic marketing prose.
3. Public brand name: **ECO-BRIDGE**. Legal/registered name, stated explicitly wherever credibility matters (footer, About/Governance page): **ECO-BRIDGE KAWEMPE**. "ECO-BRIDGE AFRICA" appears only as historical fact (founding, Jan 2025), never as current identity.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Static site only
Plain HTML/CSS, free hosting, live same day. Rejected as the final approach (not rejected outright — see recommendation) because every future content update would require a coding session, and the Secretary has no way to self-serve updates despite being constitutionally responsible for them.

### Approach B: Static site + lightweight CMS
Same fast/free static site, plus a password-protected admin panel (Decap CMS) so a non-technical board member can edit pages and add reports without code.

### Approach C: No-code platform (WordPress etc.)
Maximum editing freedom, but $5–15/month recurring cost — real money against UGX 25,000 membership fees — and less control over the verification-first information architecture.

## Recommended Approach

**B — Static site + lightweight CMS.** Free forever (matches budget reality), fast, keeps long-term maintenance with the Secretary per the Constitution rather than permanently with a developer.

## Content Source of Truth

Governance documents already professionally edited this session and committed to `governance-docs/`: Constitution, Work Plan 2026, Activity Report (+ 10 activity photos). Financial Policy and HR Policy are explicitly **excluded** from the site — they describe a paid-staff/Board-of-Directors structure that doesn't match the actual 7-person volunteer Executive Committee, and are being kept as future-state planning documents until rebuilt to match reality.

## Open Questions

- Work Plan's monthly activity timeline grid was reconstructed from stated timelines elsewhere in the document (original marks were lost in an earlier file conversion) — needs Project Management Committee sign-off before treating as authoritative.
- KCCA registration is still pending — site copy should reflect "registration in progress" language rather than "registered" until a certificate is confirmed issued.

## Success Criteria

A visitor (funder, KCCA officer, or partner) can, within one minute of landing on the site: (1) confirm the organization's legal name and registration status, (2) read or download the Constitution and Work Plan, (3) see evidence of real activity (Activity Report + photos), (4) identify the Executive Committee by name. The Secretary can publish a content update without a developer.

## Distribution Plan

Static site on free hosting (Netlify or Vercel), deployed from this git repo. CMS (Decap) commits content changes back to the same repo, redeploying automatically.
